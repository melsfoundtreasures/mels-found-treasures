Reseller Facebook Helper

Desktop Chrome extension · Version 0.1.0 · Preview

This first version fills a Marketplace draft for you to review. It does not publish, remove listings, detect sales or change stock. Facebook’s live form still needs to be verified with your account. English-language forms are supported initially.

Install once

  1. Download the extension ZIP.
  2. Right-click the ZIP in Downloads and choose Extract All. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent.
  3. In desktop Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  4. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  6. Use Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu to pin Reseller — Facebook Marketplace Helper. Refresh Reseller after installing.

Crosslist an item

  1. Open your item in Reseller. In the left toolbar select Facebook listing.
  2. Enter a description and check the asking price. Save Item first if you want to keep your changes, then reopen the item.
  3. Click Send to Facebook. Sign in to Facebook yourself if needed. Wait for its Item for sale form to load.
  4. Click the pinned Reseller extension and review the item shown. Click Fill listing in this Facebook tab.
  5. Check the fields and uploaded photos. Choose category, condition, location and delivery options yourself. Review the listing and publish it on Facebook.
  6. Open the published item’s page. Click the extension, confirm the URL belongs to your item, and choose Send listing link back to Reseller. In the Reseller tab that opens, click Save Item.

What to expect

If fields cannot be identified or already contain different text, the helper leaves them alone. You can copy the details from the popup manually. If a photo cannot transfer, add it yourself. Private photo links normally expire after an hour; sending the item again from a newly opened Reseller item renews them. Up to 10 photos can transfer.

When an item sells, record its sale in Reseller and remove any remaining Marketplace listings yourself. This helper does not monitor Facebook sales or delist items automatically.

Privacy and updates

Only the item you choose is sent: its item ID, title, price, description and photo links. The extension stores one draft and remembered listing links in this Chrome profile. It does not collect Facebook passwords, read browser cookies, or receive Supabase/Etsy credentials. Its Clear button removes the local draft and remembered links.

To update an unpacked extension, replace its files, click Reload on its card at chrome://extensions, and refresh Reseller. This preview is not published in the Chrome Web Store.
